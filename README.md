# B2B Account Profiling & Market Intelligence

### Manufacturing & Industrial Automation — Pune

> **B2B market research and account profiling project focused on identifying target manufacturing companies, mapping senior decision-makers, validating prospect data, and creating a structured dataset for targeted account-based outreach.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **B2B account profiling and market intelligence** within Pune's manufacturing and industrial automation ecosystem.

The objective was to identify relevant organizations based on predefined **industry, geography, employee-size, function, and seniority criteria**, and then map the appropriate decision-makers within those organizations.

The project follows an end-to-end **data prospecting and research workflow** — from defining the target market and discovering accounts to cleaning, validating, enriching, segmenting, and structuring the final dataset.

### Business Scenario

An industrial technology event required a highly targeted database of manufacturing and industrial automation organizations in Pune.

Instead of building a generic lead list, the research focused on identifying:

- Relevant target accounts
- Senior decision-makers
- Specific business functions
- Appropriate company-size segments
- Manufacturing verticals
- CRM-ready prospect records

The final output was structured to support **focused B2B outreach and account-based campaign activation**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The core business question was:

> **How can we identify the right manufacturing accounts in Pune and map the right decision-makers within those accounts for targeted B2B outreach?**

The research was structured around:

- **Geography:** Pune & Pune Metropolitan Region
- **Company Type:** Manufacturing & Industrial Automation
- **Target Functions:** Procurement, Supply Chain, Plant Operations, Quality & Digital Manufacturing
- **Seniority:** CXO, VP & Head of Function
- **Employee Size:** Targeted employee-size account bands

## 🔍 Research Methodology

The project followed a structured **B2B data prospecting and validation workflow**.

### **01 — Brief & Criteria Definition**

Defined the research scope using:

- Geography
- Industry
- Employee-size bands
- Business functions
- Seniority
- Account targeting criteria

### **02 — Account & Contact Discovery**

Applied targeted filters across professional B2B research platforms to identify relevant companies and senior decision-makers.

### **03 — Data Cleaning & Standardization**

Processed the collected records in Microsoft Excel and performed:

- Duplicate removal
- Job-title standardization
- Functional categorization
- Seniority classification
- Account tiering
- Data structuring

### **04 — Validation & Enrichment**

Cross-validated firmographic and contact information using multiple research and enrichment sources to improve data quality and completeness.

### **05 — CRM-Ready Structuring**

Organized the final records into a standardized structure suitable for:

- CRM upload
- Team circulation
- Account segmentation
- Targeted outreach

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Procurement & Sourcing represented the largest functional segment**, with 840 contacts (30%), followed by Supply Chain & Logistics with 700 contacts (25%).

- **Head-of-Function contacts formed the largest seniority group**, with 1,400 contacts (50%), while VP and CXO contacts represented 30% and 20% respectively.

- **The 200–500 employee segment contained the largest share of target accounts**, with 315 companies (45%), followed by 501–1,000 employees with 245 accounts (35%).

- **The database mapped multiple decision-making functions within each organization**, resulting in an average of four senior contacts per account across the 700-account dataset.

- **Seven manufacturing verticals were covered**, spanning automotive, industrial automation, engineering, electronics, pharma, FMCG, and Industry 4.0-related organizations.
